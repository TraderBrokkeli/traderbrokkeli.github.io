- Better to be out of a position wishing you were in a position than in a position wishing you were out of it.
- The market doesn't care what you want.
- Less is more, don't overcomplicate.
- Be like water. The earlier you can adapt to what the market is doing, the better it is for you.
- Don't aim to be right (e.g. timing top / bottom), aim to be on the right side of the market (e.g. showing strength).
- Don't risk what you have for what you don't need.
- Why pay for unlimited gains potential when the stock is not going to infinity.
- Desensitize yourself to the outcome. What is your thesis? Vol should increase. Ok then if vol is not increasing as the event approaches then you should exit the trade cause your thesis is wrong
- Be patient with compounding
- Good habits and processes (e.g. tracking your trades) over arbitrary goals.
