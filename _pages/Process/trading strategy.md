On “regimes” (you don’t need fancy labels)

You don’t need macro buzzwords. You only need to recognize 3 practical regimes:

1️⃣ IV Expansion / Event-driven

Earnings, FOMC, macro fear

IV elevated across chain
👉 Sell premium, take profits fast

2️⃣ Grind / Range

Price chops

IV medium, decaying slowly
👉 Hold longer, let theta work

3️⃣ Trend / Panic

One-directional moves

IV rising with price
👉 Avoid naked short premium or go very small

That’s it. Don’t overcomplicate this.
