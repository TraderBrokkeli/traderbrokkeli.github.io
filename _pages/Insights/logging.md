Article on how to properly log trades.
- 1 variable to test, everything else must be constant.
- logging inconsistently is better than no logging but we want to make it better so that we can take actionable lessons away from it.. 
- what I did, log at diff deltas, diff dte etc. useless. How do I actually study this?  E.g. 0.2 delta on a high IV stock is not gonna perform the same as that on a low IV stock. Just because it's 0.2 delta and it's your most profitable delta doesn't mean you should always sell 0.2 deltas.
- create a feedback loop
- take lower DTE trades cause 1) more trades means more data and 2) easier to roll out. But the risk is higher gamma. I'd do 15 dte and cut by 7 latest.
