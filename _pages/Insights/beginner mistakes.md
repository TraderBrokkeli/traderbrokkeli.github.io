5. Trading Notes #1: Small Edges, Big Mistakes

Purpose: Create a sustainable posting format

Structure:
3–5 short sections like:

Something you learned
Something you got wrong
Something you’re questioning
Something you’re testing

Example:

“Delta is not probability — I keep forgetting this in practice”
“I sized too big despite knowing better”
“Selling puts into weakness feels good until it doesn’t”

👉 Low effort, high consistency — this is what keeps your blog alive
